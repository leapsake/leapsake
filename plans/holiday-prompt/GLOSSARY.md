# Holiday prompt

The once-a-year question that asks what you want to do for a holiday, ahead of it.

## Language

**Holiday prompt**:
The one question per holiday occurrence asking what you will do for it, covering both your own
errands and those for the people who observe it.
_Avoid_: per-observance prompt, holiday nudge

**Hidden holiday**:
A holiday you've told Leapsake to keep out of sight because it's painful or objectionable to
you. It is suppressed everywhere.
_Avoid_: muted, irrelevant

**Relevant holiday**:
A holiday someone you know, you included, observes, whether implicitly or explicitly. Only a
relevant holiday has a prompt. Relevance is derived and is never set by hand.
_Avoid_: followed, active, enabled holiday

**Don't ask again**:
A holiday or milestone whose prompt you've switched off. What it has already scheduled still
happens.
_Avoid_: hide, mute
