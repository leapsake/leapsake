# Bulk-import dedup — glossary

**Attach**
: To bring an incoming contact into a person who is already stored, instead of creating a new
person from it. The person keeps everything they had and gains what the contact adds. Distinct
from a **merge**, which joins two stored people.
_Avoid_: merge, dedupe (for this act).

**Compatible**
: An incoming contact and a stored person are compatible when nothing either of them says
contradicts the other. Compatibility alone never attaches; it also needs **agreement**.

**Conflict**
: Two values that cannot both be true of one person: different birthdays, different first
names, or different values under the same label.

**Agreement**
: What an attach needs on top of compatibility: the same full name, and at least one more value
both share.

**Link**
: The record that a person came from a particular contact in the phone's address book. A
linked contact is never imported again, even after the person is deleted.
