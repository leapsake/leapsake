Leapsake is a private address book with a memory: the people and pets in your life,
how they are related, the dates that matter, and reminders that reach you before them.

Everything is stored on your phone. There is no server and nothing is sent to us — you
can use every part of this build without signing in anywhere or giving us an email
address. Creating an account is optional, happens entirely on this device, and is what
turns on encryption (step 6). Nothing is locked behind it: if a screen looks like it is
waiting for a login before you have made one, that is a bug; please report it.

Please try, in roughly this order:

1. Open the app and add the first person. The home screen nudges you.
2. Add a second person, then relate the two (partner, parent, sibling — anything).
   Check the relationship reads correctly from both people's pages.
3. Put a birthday or an anniversary on someone, then reopen the app and confirm it is
   still there and that a reminder appears ahead of it.
4. Write a reminder that @mentions a person and carries a #tag, then find it again by
   that tag.
5. Allow notifications when asked, and see whether the reminder actually arrives.
6. Optionally: create an account in Settings. It stays on this device — it turns on
   encryption and gives you a recovery phrase. Write the phrase down.

New in this build:

- Gifts: paste a shop link and Leapsake names the gift and shows its picture. It fetches
  that page directly from your phone, with no server of ours in between.
- Birthdays and anniversaries now ask each year what you’d like to do, such as give a gift
  or send a card, and you can tell them not to ask again.
- Possible duplicates are reviewed one pair at a time, and you choose which one to keep.
- Phone numbers work with or without a country code, including for WhatsApp and Signal.
- Notifications arrive as one daily digest, and can be turned on per device.
- Most screens have been tidied: one style of button, menu and sheet throughout.

Things we know are unfinished: syncing between devices is switched off in this build,
photos are not in yet, and the typography and icons are placeholders.

Please do not keep anything here that you have no other copy of. This is an early beta,
and recovering a store whose key is lost is not something we have finished testing yet
— a device restore or an iOS reset can take the key with it.

Tell us anything that felt slow, confusing, or wrong, however small.
