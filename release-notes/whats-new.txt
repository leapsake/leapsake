Share a web page to Leapsake from Chrome or any app, and it becomes a gift idea.

Gifts: paste a shop link and Leapsake fills in the name and picture.

Birthdays and anniversaries now ask each year what you’d like to do — a gift, a card, or nothing.

Duplicates are reviewed one pair at a time, and you choose which to keep.

Phone numbers work with or without a country code, including for WhatsApp and Signal.

Reminders arrive as one daily digest, and most screens have been tidied throughout.
