Leapsake is a private address book with a memory: the people and pets in your life, how
they are related, the dates that matter, and reminders that reach you before them.

Everything stays on your phone. There is no server to sign in to, and nothing is sent to
us. Creating an account is optional and happens entirely on the device — it is what turns
on encryption for everything Leapsake stores.

This is the first release.
